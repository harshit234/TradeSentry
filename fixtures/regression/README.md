# Sprint 9 regression fixtures

`cases_a_n.json` is the immutable, pre-test contract for the fourteen labeled
evaluation cases. It was authored before `tests/evaluation/test_cases_a_n.py`.

Inputs contain structured facts and adversarial strings only. They are synthetic,
contain no customer data, and do not authorize settlement. Expected risk outputs
are prototype investigation signals; every consequential action remains subject
to human approval.
